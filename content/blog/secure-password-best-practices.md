# The Weakness of "Password123"

Data breaches are happening every day. If you reuse passwords or use simple phrases, you are at risk. A strong password is your first line of defense against hackers.

## What Makes a Password Strong?

1.  **Length**: At least 12 characters (longer is better).
2.  **Complexity**: A mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.
3.  **Unpredictability**: Random strings are harder to guess than dictionary words.
