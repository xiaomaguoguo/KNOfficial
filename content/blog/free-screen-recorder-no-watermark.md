# The Best Free Screen Recorder with No Watermark (Unlimited Time)

## The Problem with Free Recorders

Most "free" screen recorders come with a catch. They slap a giant watermark across your video, making it unusable for professional presentations or YouTube.

Others, like the free tier of Loom, limit you to just 5 minutes of recording time. And almost all of them require you to install heavy software that slows down your computer.

## The Solution: KNothing Screen Recorder

We built the **[KNothing Screen Recorder](/screen-recorder)** to be different. It uses the native browser API (`getDisplayMedia`) to capture your screen directly.

*   **Zero Bloat**: Just open the URL and click Record. No installation needed.
*   **Privacy**: Your video is processed in your browser's memory. It is never uploaded to the cloud.

## Key Features

*   ✅ **No Watermark**: Get a clean video for professional use, completely free.
*   ✅ **MP4 Support**: Native MP4 recording on Chrome and Edge (with smart fallback to WebM on Safari).
*   ✅ **System Audio**: Capture your computer's sound along with the screen.

1.  Go to **[Screen Recorder](/screen-recorder)**.
