# What is a Cron Job?

In the world of Linux and Unix, **cron** is a time-based job scheduler. Users use cron to schedule jobs (commands or scripts) to run periodically at fixed times, dates, or intervals.

## The Syntax Nightmare

A standard cron expression looks like this:
`5 4 * * *`

Do you know what that means? It means "At 04:05." But remembering the order of fields (Minute, Hour, Day, Month, Weekday) is a headache for even experienced developers.
