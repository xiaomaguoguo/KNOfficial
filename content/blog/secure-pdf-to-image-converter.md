# How to Convert Sensitive PDFs to Images Safely (No Upload Required)

We've all been there: You need to turn a PDF contract, invoice, or bank statement into an image (JPG or PNG) to upload it somewhere.

You search online for "PDF to JPG converter," and you find dozens of free tools. But then you pause.

**"Is it safe to upload this confidential document to a random server?"**

The answer is usually: You don't know. Server-side converters store your file temporarily to process it. Even if they promise to delete it, there's always a risk of data breaches or "shadow" copies.

## How It Works (Technical Trust)

We use **PDF.js**, a battle-tested open-source library built by Mozilla (the creators of Firefox).

When you drop a file into our tool:
1.  The browser reads the file into memory.
2.  PDF.js renders each page onto a hidden HTML5 Canvas.
3.  We convert that canvas into a high-quality JPG or PNG blob.
4.  You download the result.

No servers. No cloud processing. No risk.
