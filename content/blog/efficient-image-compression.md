# The Heavy Cost of Large Images

Did you know that images often account for more than 60% of a webpage's total weight? Large, unoptimized images are the #1 killer of website speed.

## Why Speed Matters

1.  **User Experience**: 53% of mobile users abandon sites that take longer than 3 seconds to load.
2.  **SEO**: Google uses page speed as a ranking factor. Faster sites rank higher.
3.  **Bandwidth**: Smaller images save data for your users and bandwidth costs for you.

## Lossy vs. Lossless

- **Lossless**: Reduces file size without removing any image data. Great for text and simple graphics.
- **Lossy**: Removes some data that the human eye can't perceive. Perfect for photographs, often reducing size by 80-90%.
